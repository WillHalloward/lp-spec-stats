Stage one · Zul’jan

Spinning axes, stage one

A tick is one Axegrinder hit; standing in an axe ticks every quarter-second, so an axe eaten is a run of ticks with no gap longer than 0.75s. Only ticks on a living player before the wipe count, and everything is per minute alive, so a pull where people died early is not flattered. 6613 ticks, 1579 axes, 5.3 ticks per minute alive.

Axes pile up through the stage, so the later seconds are worth more ticks. vs night divides what a player or pull took by what the night’s average would give for the same seconds of the stage (10-second slices): 1.0 is the night average, 2.0 is twice it. Against that, the raid took 0.94× the night average in the first half of the night and 1.05× in the second. Both tanks sit at the top: they stand where Zul’jan is, and the table does not know whether the axes route through there, so read their rows against each other rather than against the raid.

The explosions

A purple touching another orb puts 20 Venom Rupture stacks on everyone at once. Frontals add stacks on their cast and the push adds them as stage two starts, so a jump of 15 or more, or a gain at any other moment, is an explosion. On a raid with no stacks the log records the explosion without a count (2 of them), so those show as “–”; killed is the Venom Rupture deaths in the next two and a half seconds. One within 1.5s of a Toxic Deluge cast, with nobody carrying, is a Mutation left on the ground; the rest were touched. Holding a purple lists anyone carrying one, or who put one down, in the 3 seconds before. That is who was near the purple, not proof of who touched it: the log does not carry positions here.

Orbs per frontal

Each Sever detonates the orbs in it and puts one Venom Rupture stack per orb on the raid, so the stacks are the orb count. Columns are the Severs in order; a column of seven or more at the end (145–162s) is the push into stage two, where every orb left goes off at once. Explosions are not in this grid.

Guillotine soaks

One player is marked, and the axe lands on them five seconds later; everyone it hits shares it. If too few are under it, it becomes Execution on the whole raid (a miss). Two per stage one, at 48s and 133s; Grim Guillotine in stage three. 49 soaks before a wipe, 2 missed; the median soak had 9 players and none with 6 or more failed. Of the 6 misses over the night, those after the wipe had started are a raid that had already fallen apart, not a cause.

The shield burn

Malacrass puts up Veil of Twilight, a 34.8M absorb, and channels Eternal Nightfall. Break the absorb and the channel stops; if not, the cast finishes about seventeen seconds in and kills most of the raid. 9 of 13 broken (fastest 10.7s, slowest 16.6s); the closest miss was pull 13: 94% of it. Shields that went up after the wipe had started are left out. Into the shield is every hit on Malacrass while it was up, absorbed or not; pets bill to their owner.

How this is read. A wipe starts at the first death of the first run of 4 deaths inside 8 seconds that the raid never recovers from (no 20-second stretch without a death before the pull ends); nothing after it counts unless a table says so. Alive: a player is dead from their death until they next cast something, so a battle rez brings them back. Reset pulls (5) carry Malacrass’s Unworthy and end inside 35 seconds; they are left out. Stage three was reached once, so its Grim Guillotine, Blighted Sever and shield sit in the tables above with their stage marked rather than in a section of their own.
