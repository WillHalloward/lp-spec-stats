The burn windows

Rage of the Shackled exposed the Venomous Heart 28 times across the night, always for the full 20 seconds — the raid never lost a window early. Everything the raid puts out in those 20 seconds counts, because the boss stays cleavable: the heart takes double damage, Ula'tek herself takes normal damage, and both are part of the burn. Across the night that split is 77% heart, 23% boss. First windows landed 207.2M on average, second windows 137.6M. That 34% gap is mostly bought, not earned: the lust went out 4 to 19 seconds before the first exposure on every pull that reached one, and nothing is up for the second. A player's own drop is only worth reading against the raid's, which is what the table further down does.

Left and right

Ten seconds after the first heart closes, the raid splits: two fixed tens, one to each side of the platform, thousands of units apart in the log's coordinates and unmistakable in the position data. The phase opens at 165–170s on every pull and runs 45 to 110 seconds, and the boss cannot be touched until about 110s into it — which is the only deadline that matters for getting back. Each half carries one tank and two healers, and the same twenty names went to the same two sides on all nineteen pulls that got there.

Defensives in the heavy seconds

The log's own damage curve marks the dangerous parts of a pull: 121 spans across the night where raid damage taken ran at twice the pull's median or more. They are 12% of the time on the boss and 22% of the damage. This is who pressed something in them.

Two readings to take with salt. Throatclogga leans on short-cooldown mitigation rather than saves — 571 casts of Purifying Brew, which does not count as a major cooldown here. Backtospawn is the opposite case: 194 majors is a Blood rotation. The names to look at are the ones with a full kit and a low bar: Ragonzsham, Iylenvur and Ziekil.

Healthstones & health potions

190 healthstones and 133 health potions went down across 21 pulls — and they went down late rather than early: 203 of the 323 at under 40% health, 66 of those under 20%, with only 16 above 80%. Average 37%, median 35%. Four never touched a healthstone at all (Blàdee, Friedchicken, Ragonzsham and Sulul). The potion count is every health potion on the shared cooldown: 95 Concentrated Silvermoon Health Potion, 30 Silvermoon Health Potion and 8 Potent Healing Potion; the stones are 127 Healthstone and 63 Demonic Healthstone.

Early deaths are the 152 deaths that landed more than 15 seconds before the pull's last death — someone dying alone rather than in the wipe. 139 of them had no major defensive in the ten seconds before, and 232 deaths happened in pulls where that player never used a stone or a potion.

Heavy spans are computed, not hand-picked: raid damage taken binned per second, and every run of three or more seconds at twice the pull's median rate or above. Necrotic Vapors, Blight Vein and Acidic Expulsion dominate them. Majors are personal cooldowns (Anti-Magic Shell, Aspect of the Turtle, Astral Shift, Barkskin, Blur, Cloak of Shadows, Dark Pact, Divine Protection, Divine Shield, Evasion, Exhilaration, Fortifying Brew, Greater Invisibility, Ice Cold, Icebound Fortitude, Lichborne, Obsidian Scales, Renewing Blaze, Shield of Vengeance, Survival of the Fittest, Unending Resolve, Vampiric Blood). Minor covers the spammables — Alter Time, Black Ox Brew, Crimson Vial, Dancing Rune Weapon, Expel Harm, Feint, Frenzied Regeneration, Prismatic Barrier, Purifying Brew. Stones and potions are counted from the cast, not the heal, so an overheal still counts as a use. Health at use is the value before the heal landed: WCL stamps the health on both the cast and the heal event as the figure after healing (casts that read 100% health while reporting real, non-overhealed healing give it away), so the effective heal is subtracted back off. Externals are the ones cast on somebody else: Anti-Magic Zone, Aura Mastery, Blessing of Protection, Blessing of Sacrifice, Darkness, Ironbark, Lay on Hands, Spirit Link Totem, Zephyr.

How this was counted

Burn damage is every damage event inside an exposure window landing on either the Venomous Heart or Ula'tek herself, absorbs included, with pet and guardian damage credited to the owner. The heart takes 100% increased damage, so a hit parked there is worth two of the same hit on the boss — the “on heart” column is how much of a player's burn went to the target that pays double. Exposure windows were rebuilt by clustering those events and breaking at gaps over five seconds; all 28 came out at 20 seconds, against 30 Rage of the Shackled casts minus the two pulls that died before the first exposure.

Wave hits are damage events from Caustic Waves (spell 1292211, 1292403, 1298570, 1311807 and 1319282), grouped per player when they land within two seconds of each other, so one wave passing through a player counts once. Wave casts count the boss's Caustic Waves casts. A wave death is a death whose killing blow is the wave itself — damage taken and then dying to something else a second later is not counted here.

Viper counts are distinct Blightscale Viper instances that took damage in a pull, a proxy for eggs hatched wrong; on this night they track the early pulls more than the deep ones.
