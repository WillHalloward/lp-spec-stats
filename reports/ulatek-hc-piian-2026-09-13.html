The burn windows

Rage of the Shackled exposed the Venomous Heart 27 times. Every one ran the full 20 seconds; no window was lost early. The boss stays cleavable through an exposure, so both targets count: the heart takes double damage, Ula'tek takes normal. Across the night the split is 76% heart, 24% boss. First windows landed 208.7M on average, second windows 83.4M. The 60% drop from the first window to the second tracks lust, which went out 3 to 17 seconds before the first exposure on every pull that reached one and is not up again for the second.

Damage onto the boss

Caustic Waves

195 waves went out; 92 of them clipped somebody, and 9 of those hits killed. Tank contacts are the cost of holding Ula'tek in melee; the other 65 are the avoidable ones.

The boss throws them in two phases. Phase one is two volleys of about four waves a pull, 192 casts across the night, catching non-tanks 63 times, 0.33 a cast. Phase two is 3 casts on the one pull that reached it, 2 hits, 0.67 a cast. Deaths split 8 to 1. Phase one accounts for 63 of the 65 non-tank hits and 8 of the 9 deaths.

Left and right

The raid splits ten seconds after the first heart closes, into two groups either side of the platform, thousands of coordinate units apart. The phase opens at 160 to 175s on every pull and runs 30 to 105 seconds; the boss is untargetable until about 110s in, which is the deadline for getting back. Across the 21 pulls that reached it the sides were west 10 (1 tank, 2 healers, 7 damage) and east 10 (1 tank, 2 healers, 7 damage). One player moved to the other side partway through, at pull 4, so each pull is read on its own. Four pulls had somebody on the far side of their own assignment (pull 3, pull 5, pull 11 and pull 24), which is what a wrong turn looks like. Deaths inside the phase are listed per player.

The sides are not comparable on damage this night. West is handed 227 adds across the night against east's 113, 11 a pull against 5. Most of the difference is one add: west takes 120 Blightscale Rawlings to east's 62. The 56.9/43.1 phase damage split measures that workload, not throughput: night-wide the two sides are 51.2/48.8. Per add the order reverses, 6.4M west against 9.7M east, because east's are the larger targets. Compare them on deaths and on getting back to the middle instead.

Defensives in the heavy seconds

111 spans across the night ran at twice the pull's median damage taken or more. They are 13% of the time on the boss and 27% of the damage. This is who pressed something in them.

Two bars that don't mean what they look like. The Frost bar sits low because that spec mitigates with a short cooldown rather than a saved one: 184 casts of Ice Barrier, which is not a major by this count. The Blood bar is the opposite, where 224 majors is a rotation rather than 224 saves.

Healthstones & health potions

195 healthstones and 136 health potions went down across 25 pulls, and they went down late rather than early: 186 of the 331 at under 40% health, 56 of those under 20%, 13 above 80%. Average 39%, median 37%. Two of the roster used no healthstone at all; the chart marks them. The potion count is every health potion on the shared cooldown: 71 Concentrated Silvermoon Health Potion, 48 Silvermoon Health Potion and 17 Potent Healing Potion; the stones are 134 Healthstone and 61 Demonic Healthstone.

Early deaths are the 206 deaths that landed more than 15 seconds before the pull's last death, meaning someone dying alone rather than in the wipe. 156 of them had no major defensive in the ten seconds before, and 300 deaths happened in pulls where that player never used a stone or a potion.

Heavy spans are computed, not hand-picked: raid damage taken binned per second, and every run of three or more seconds at twice the pull's median rate or above. Necrotic Vapors, Blight Vein and Acidic Expulsion dominate them. Majors are personal cooldowns (Anti-Magic Shell, Astral Shift, Barkskin, Blur, Dark Pact, Desperate Prayer, Die by the Sword, Dispersion, Divine Protection, Divine Shield, Exhilaration, Fortifying Brew, Greater Invisibility, Ice Cold, Icebound Fortitude, Lichborne, Metamorphosis, Rallying Cry, Shield Wall, Shield of Vengeance, Survival of the Fittest, Unending Resolve, Vampiric Blood). Minor covers the spammables: Alter Time, Blazing Barrier, Dancing Rune Weapon, Frenzied Regeneration, Ice Barrier, Prismatic Barrier. Stones and potions are counted from the cast, not the heal, so an overheal still counts as a use. Health at use is the value before the heal landed: WCL stamps the health on both the cast and the heal event as the figure after healing (casts that read 100% health while reporting real, non-overhealed healing give it away), so the effective heal is subtracted back off. Externals are the ones cast on somebody else: Anti-Magic Zone, Blessing of Protection, Darkness, Ironbark, Lay on Hands, Life Cocoon, Spirit Link Totem.

How this was counted

Burn damage is every damage event inside an exposure window landing on either the Venomous Heart or Ula'tek herself, absorbs included, with pet and guardian damage credited to the owner. The heart takes 100% increased damage, so a hit parked there is worth two of the same hit on the boss. The “on heart” column is how much of a player's burn went to the target that pays double. Exposure windows were rebuilt by clustering those events and breaking at gaps over five seconds; all 27 came out at 20 seconds, against 27 Rage of the Shackled casts minus the four pulls that died before the first exposure.

Wave hits are damage events from Caustic Waves (spell 1292211, 1292403, 1298570, 1311807 and 1319282), grouped per player when they land within two seconds of each other, so one wave passing through a player counts once. Wave casts count the boss's Caustic Waves casts. A wave death is a death whose killing blow is the wave itself. Damage taken and then dying to something else a second later is not counted here. Phase one is everything before the split phase and phase two everything after it, the split phase being dated from the coordinates as described below; casts within ten seconds of each other count as one volley.

Viper counts are distinct Blightscale Viper instances that took damage in a pull, a proxy for eggs hatched wrong; on this night they track the early pulls more than the deep ones.
