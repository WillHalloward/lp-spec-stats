  • Progress. Best pull 30.6% on pull 7 (Vexhul 25%, Ithraz 36%). On the pulls that reached Submerge, Ithraz ended a median 12 points higher than Vexhul. Median boss health at the wipe went from 59.0% in pulls 1–4 to 36.1% in pulls 5–9.

  • Deaths feed the wipe. 3 wipes began with a globule death (2 soaking one, 1 an explosion); in 3 of them another player had died in the 15 seconds before, and the globules a dead player drops have to be soaked too. Vile Flood killed 2 players before a wipe, in 2 pulls.

The night on one clock

Every pull of Mythic Twin Fangs runs to the same script, so every pull can share one time axis, running to the length of the longest pull (280 seconds). A pull's row ends where it ended. The wipe starts at the first death of the first run of 4 deaths within 8 seconds. Deaths from there on are drawn small and faded and are not held against anyone. Pick a pull to see it player by player, with soaks, kicks and Feast hits.

Where the pulls broke: the first Feast and its founts (pulls 3 and 6), the second Feast (pull 1) and after Submerge (pulls 2, 4, 5, 7, 8 and 9). What started the wipes, by the first death of each: Caustic Globule soak (2), Ravenous Feast (1), tank damage (1), Visceral Burst from a missed kick (1), Caustic Globule explosion (1), Stone Breaker (1), the snakes (1) and Eternal Venom ticks (1). 7 pulls reached the second Rouse.

How the fight runs

Timings are from this log. Both bosses are fought together, and Eternal Venom stacks tie the mechanics together.

Blood Torrent & globules (9s, 70s)Vexhul's Caustic Deluge drops 15 Caustic Globules over five seconds, and Ithraz's spilled blood wraps most of them (13 to 15 a wave this night) in a Barbed Bulwark. Crowd control strips the shield, and one player walks into each globule for one Eternal Venom stack. An unsoaked globule explodes and gives everybody a stack.
Rouse the Brood (36s, 97s)Ten Broodlings surface one by one, five per side, on ten fixed spots in an arc, and start casting Visceral Burst. In the second Rouse at a boss position a spot can be used twice. A kick sends one back into the water. A completed Burst hits the whole raid hard and leaves a stacking DoT.
Stone Breaker (24s, 86s)Ithraz's Stone Breaker lands three times in about seven seconds. It killed 4 players before or at the start of a wipe and started 1 wipe.
Stir the Depths (47s, 108s)Waves cross the room; every one that catches a player gives a stack of Eternal Venom. Waves were 8% of the night’s stacks.
Venomous Emergence (with Rouse)Three Spawn of Vexhul appear and give everyone a stack of Eternal Venom. Each one keeps casting Corrosive Spit lines that add stacks until it dies.
Ravenous Feast (61s, 122s)Three smashes, each split by at least four soakers. Every soak removes one Eternal Venom stack, and a player cannot soak two smashes of one Feast. A smash with too few in it goes out to the whole raid. Blood founts follow and have to be healed into within eight seconds, or Tainted Burst goes off on the raid.
Eternal VenomA permanent stacking DoT. Its ticks grow with the stacks, and guides put the one-shot at nine stacks on Mythic. A player who dies drops up to three globules that have to be soaked, so one death feeds the next.
Submerge (140s)Sanguine Storm, then Vexhul's Vile Flood beam sweeps the room. Every beam tick is a stack. 6 pulls reached it before the wipe.
After SubmergeThe bosses surface in a corner and the cycle starts again: globules at 164s, Rouse at 191s and Feast at 216s, read off pull 7. The Rouse spots move with the bosses.

Rouse the Brood: who kicked what

22 Rouses before a wipe, with 217 Broodlings. 6 Broodlings got a Visceral Burst off, in 5 of the 22 Rouses; the other 17 had none. Nothing after a wipe started is counted. Half of the 6 went off 11.0 seconds or more after the Rouse began. 4 of the 6 came in a second Rouse at a boss position (Rouse 2 or 4). Broodlings went off most often at R2 (1), R1 (1), L3 (1), L2 (1), L5 (1) and L1 (1).

Fixed spots. A Broodling is placed at the nearest of the ten spots by where it first took damage. 23 were kicked before anything hit them, so the log has no position for them. Sides always alternate, so their side is known. 9 of them could also be placed, because only one spot fits the spawn rules (the first Rouse at each boss position, before Submerge and again in the corner after it, uses every spot once). The other 14 are known by side only. Kicking the same spot most of the night (60% or more): Vesemirr (L5, 60%), Mattiseppo (L4, 70%), Aelyssar (L3, 68%) and Indris (R3, 67%).

Was a kick available? For each Broodling that went off, the table at the end of this section lists every kicker who was alive with their interrupt off cooldown. “On that side” means a kicker who normally kicks the side the Broodling was on. For all 6, at least one such kicker was ready. The 2 Broodlings that started a wipe: L3 on pull 4 (Mattiseppo and Aelyssar’s pair) and L2 on pull 4 (Piiaan and Niydra’s pair). The log has no range check, so “ready” means the cooldown was up, not that the Broodling was in reach.

One row per Rouse, spots left to right as in the room. A cell is who kicked that Broodling. Where the second Rouse doubles up. The first Rouse at each boss position puts one Broodling on every spot; the second can reuse one, but only from three or more Broodlings back on that side, so only the 4th and 5th on a side ever land on a used spot. Across 80 Mythic kills a side only ever runs one of the five sequences below (see Rouse spawn pattern). This night 4 of the 8 sides read in full had a repeat. 10 other sides could not be read in full: 8 had a Broodling with no spot and 2 were cut short by the wipe. Tags read pull and side: P3 L is the left side of pull 3’s second Rouse.

Ravenous Feast and the founts

Soaks. 19 Feasts before a wipe. 1 smash went out to the whole raid because too few were in it. The Feast that started a wipe had a smash go out to the raid (players hit per smash: pull 1 4 · all 17). A smash went to the raid or a Tainted Burst went off in 0 of 9 first Feasts, 1 of 7 second and 0 of 3 third. Everyone soaked at least three in four of the Feasts they were alive for. Alfi took 29 globule-soak stacks and skipped 4 of 19 Feasts; Feast soaks are the only way to shed stacks. Every soak takes one Eternal Venom stack off, so a skipped Feast is a stack kept. Players who die in a smash that went to the raid are often the ones who did go in; they count in the deaths below.

Globules, shields and Eternal Venom

6 globules exploded unsoaked before a wipe: pulls 3 and 4 in wave 1; pulls 3 and 6 in wave 2; pulls 5 and 9 in wave 3. The shields were not the problem: 360 of the 363 Barbed Bulwarks were stripped by crowd control, mostly by Paladins (67), Warriors (48) and Shamans (42). Another 11 went off after a wipe had started, mostly the globules dead players drop: a cost of wiping, not a soak problem.

Each globule is one stack for one player. With about 20 alive and 15 globules a wave, about 5 people go without one in every wave by design, so these lists only mean something when the same name keeps coming up. Tanks are left out. Most important — alive and without a globule in a wave where one exploded: Emberforge (4 of 6), Atheniás (3 of 6), Huibalast (3 of 6), Mattiseppo (3 of 6), Taranus (3 of 6) and Vokster (3 of 6). Soaked in under half the waves they were alive for: nobody. Took two or more in one wave: Aelyssar (3 waves), Huibalast (3 waves), Vesemirr (3 waves), Indris (2 waves) and Taranus (2 waves); usually someone covering a gap, at the cost of the extra stack.

The count. 25 globule waves came before a wipe. Each dropped 15 globules. Players who died during a wave dropped 17 more, one to three each, two seconds after the death (Шамшыммыч on pull 2; Laradendusk on pull 3; Blossy on pull 5; Vesemirr and Aelyssar on pull 7; Niydra and Indris on pull 9). Another 54 dropped outside those waves: 31 from players dying away from a wave and 23 not tied to a death. That makes 446 in all.

Where the stacks came from. 1189 stacks before a wipe: 36% from Venomous Emergence, which everyone takes, 35% from globule soaks, and 10% from globules exploding. 8% came from waves, 6% from snake spit on its target and 1% from spit cleaving someone next to the target, and Vile Flood (4%) and globules landing on someone as they dropped (2%) gave the rest. Most wave and spit-cleave stacks, from not dodging during mechanics: Valanarr (12), Taranus (11), Vesemirr (9), Alfi (8), Indris (8) and Laradendusk (8). (The spit’s target can’t dodge the beam, so their stacks are left out.) Most Vile Flood stacks, from standing in the beam during Submerge: Valanarr (8), Blossy (7), Aelyssar (4) and Huibalast (4). 2 deaths to Eternal Venom ticks before a wipe started: 2 at 9 stacks. (A Venom death that started a wipe is counted with the wipe causes above.)

Waves soaked is waves with at least one globule, not globules, so taking two in a wave doesn’t raise it. Globules per wave is wave globules soaked divided by waves alive; 15 globules over about 20 people is about 0.75 each. Death globules soaked: a dead player’s globules are extra work, not a missed soak, so they are kept out of the other columns. The log doesn’t say which globule a soak was. In a wave where someone died, a player’s 2nd or later soak after the death drop counts as a death globule, up to the number dropped. Outside a wave, any soak 2–15 s after a death counts.

Double soaks. One globule soak takes most of a health bar, so a second one inside 3 seconds, before the healers have caught up, kills. 4 players died that way, 0 of them before a wipe or as its first death. That is 0 of the 9 soak deaths before a wipe; the other 9 died to a single globule. The median gap between the two soaks was 1.1 seconds. Most: Vesemirr (1), Piiaan (1), Alfi (1) and Huibalast (1).

The snakes

22 Venomous Emergences before a wipe. All three snakes died in 19 of them, the last at a median of 13.2 seconds after they surfaced. They got off 71 Corrosive Spits, a median of 3 per Emergence. Every spit that lands is a stack: 6% of the night’s stacks were on the spit’s target, and 1% on players caught in the cleave.

How this is read. A wipe starts at the first death of the first run of 4 deaths within 8 seconds; the kill, if there is one, has none. A mechanic that opens after the wipe started is not counted, and inside one that was already running, nothing after the wipe start counts (Broodlings, Bursts, kicks, smashes, snake damage). A globule wave cut off by the wipe within 7.5 seconds (before its drops end) is not a wave; its globules are counted on their own. What started a wipe is the cause of its first death. Alive: a player is dead from their death until they next cast something, so a battle rez brings them back. A Broodling's spot is the nearest of the ten spots to where it first took damage, within seven yards. A kick ready means the kicker was alive and their interrupt's cooldown was up when the Burst completed; the night's most-used interrupt counts for each player. A globule soak is a Caustic Globule hit on one player. An explosion is the other Caustic Globule spell hitting three or more players within 0.6 seconds. A stack's source is the hit that landed from 0.35 seconds before the stack to 0.15 seconds after it. A shield strip is a Barbed Bulwark dropping within 0.4 seconds of a stun, fear or incapacitate on that globule. Healing into a fount is healing absorbed on a player while they carry Tainted Blood, leaving out anyone also under Blood Torrent's absorb.
