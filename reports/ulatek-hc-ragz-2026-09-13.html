The burn windows

Rage of the Shackled exposed the Venomous Heart 26 times across the night. Every one ran the full 20 seconds, so the raid never lost a window early. Everything the raid puts out in those 20 seconds counts, because the boss stays cleavable: the heart takes double damage, Ula'tek herself takes normal damage, and both are part of the burn. Across the night that split is 77% heart, 23% boss. First windows landed 223.4M on average, second windows 144.1M, third windows 78.8M. That 35% gap between the first two is mostly bought, not earned: the lust went out 6 to 19 seconds before the first exposure on every pull that reached one, and nothing is up for the second. A player's own drop is only worth reading against the raid's, which is what the table further down does. One pull lived long enough for a third window (pull 8), and that window is where the boss actually falls: short, with nothing left on cooldown, so it reads low and should.

Damage onto the boss

Ula'tek, Gore Rattle and the Venomous Heart share one health bar, so the only number that decides whether she dies is the sum of the three. This is every pull of the night, start to finish, not the 519 seconds of exposure the burn charts measure. 10.66B went into the pool across 16 pulls: 56% into Ula'tek herself, 35% into the heart, 8.2% into Gore Rattle. Caynlock led it with 892M. A player can carry a window and still be thin across a fight, and the two orders differ here: Blossy six places lower, Darkowlmaste four places higher and Preng three places lower than the burn windows put them.

Caustic Waves

196 waves went out; 165 of them clipped somebody, and 28 of those hits killed. Tank contacts are the cost of holding Ula'tek in melee; the other 125 are the avoidable ones.

The boss throws them on two patterns, and they are worth reading apart. Before the split phase it is 2 volleys of about 4 waves, every pull, 128 casts across the night, and they caught non-tanks 30 times — 23% of casts. After it the volleys come about every fifty seconds, up to 4 of them on the longest pulls, about 3 waves each, 68 casts on the nine pulls that got that far, catching non-tanks 95 times, or 140% of casts. Deaths follow the same shape: 2 in the first phase, 26 in the second. No wave has ever landed during the split phase itself.

Left and right

Ten seconds after the first heart closes, the raid splits: two groups, one to each side of the platform, thousands of units apart in the log's coordinates and unmistakable in the position data. The phase opens at 165–170s on every pull and runs 55 to 110 seconds, and the boss cannot be touched until about 110s into it, which is the only deadline that matters for getting back. Across the fifteen pulls that reached it the sides were west 11 (1 tank, 3 healers, 7 damage) and east 9 (1 tank, 2 healers, 6 damage). Two players moved to the other side partway through, at pull 9 and pull 11, so each pull is read on its own. No pull had anyone standing on the far side of their own assignment. Deaths out there are listed per player: that is the list to read when deciding who needs an external or a cooldown call.

The two sides were handed much the same work this night, 169 adds against 178, 11 a pull against 12, so the 49.4/50.6 damage split is a fair comparison between them. It sits against 52.3/47.7 across the night as a whole.

Defensives in the heavy seconds

The log's own damage curve marks the dangerous parts of a pull: 94 spans across the night where raid damage taken ran at twice the pull's median or more. They are 11% of the time on the boss and 21% of the damage. This is who pressed something in them.

Two bars that don't mean what they look like. The Brewmaster bar sits low because that spec mitigates with a short cooldown rather than a saved one: 490 casts of Purifying Brew, which is not a major by this count. The Blood bar is the opposite, where 159 majors is a rotation rather than 159 saves.

Healthstones & health potions

118 healthstones and 85 health potions went down across 16 pulls, and they went down late rather than early: 101 of the 203 at under 40% health, 24 of those under 20%, 13 above 80%. Average 42%, median 40%. Four of the roster used no healthstone at all; the chart marks them. The potion count is every health potion on the shared cooldown: 70 Concentrated Silvermoon Health Potion, 10 Silvermoon Health Potion and 5 Potent Healing Potion; the stones are 81 Healthstone and 37 Demonic Healthstone.

Early deaths are the 115 deaths that landed more than 15 seconds before the pull's last death, meaning someone dying alone rather than in the wipe. 93 of them had no major defensive in the ten seconds before, and 207 deaths happened in pulls where that player never used a stone or a potion.

Heavy spans are computed, not hand-picked: raid damage taken binned per second, and every run of three or more seconds at twice the pull's median rate or above. Necrotic Vapors, Blight Vein and Acidic Expulsion dominate them. Majors are personal cooldowns (Anti-Magic Shell, Aspect of the Turtle, Astral Shift, Barkskin, Blur, Cloak of Shadows, Dark Pact, Divine Protection, Divine Shield, Evasion, Exhilaration, Fortifying Brew, Greater Invisibility, Ice Cold, Icebound Fortitude, Lichborne, Obsidian Scales, Renewing Blaze, Shield of Vengeance, Survival of the Fittest, Unending Resolve, Vampiric Blood). Minor covers the spammables: Alter Time, Black Ox Brew, Crimson Vial, Dancing Rune Weapon, Expel Harm, Feint, Frenzied Regeneration, Prismatic Barrier, Purifying Brew. Stones and potions are counted from the cast, not the heal, so an overheal still counts as a use. Health at use is the value before the heal landed: WCL stamps the health on both the cast and the heal event as the figure after healing (casts that read 100% health while reporting real, non-overhealed healing give it away), so the effective heal is subtracted back off. Externals are the ones cast on somebody else: Anti-Magic Zone, Blessing of Protection, Darkness, Ironbark, Lay on Hands, Spirit Link Totem, Zephyr.

How this was counted

Burn damage is every damage event inside an exposure window landing on either the Venomous Heart or Ula'tek herself, absorbs included, with pet and guardian damage credited to the owner. The heart takes 100% increased damage, so a hit parked there is worth two of the same hit on the boss. The “on heart” column is how much of a player's burn went to the target that pays double. Exposure windows were rebuilt by clustering those events and breaking at gaps over five seconds; all 26 came out at 20 seconds, against 26 Rage of the Shackled casts minus the one pulls that died before the first exposure.

Wave hits are damage events from Caustic Waves (spell 1292211, 1292403, 1298570, 1311807 and 1319282), grouped per player when they land within two seconds of each other, so one wave passing through a player counts once. Wave casts count the boss's Caustic Waves casts. A wave death is a death whose killing blow is the wave itself. Damage taken and then dying to something else a second later is not counted here. Phase one is everything before the split phase and phase two everything after it, the split phase being dated from the coordinates as described below; casts within ten seconds of each other count as one volley.

Viper counts are distinct Blightscale Viper instances that took damage in a pull, a proxy for eggs hatched wrong; on this night they track the early pulls more than the deep ones.
