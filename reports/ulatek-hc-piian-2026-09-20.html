The burn windows

Rage of the Shackled exposed the Venomous Heart 25 times across the night. Two of them ran short, at 17 seconds and 18 seconds, because the pull ended inside the window rather than because the raid lost it. Everything the raid puts out in those 20 seconds counts, because the boss stays cleavable: the heart takes double damage, Ula'tek herself takes normal damage, and both are part of the burn. Across the night that split is 76% heart, 24% boss. First windows landed 223.2M on average, second windows 144.5M, third windows 38.3M. That 35% gap between the first two is mostly bought, not earned: the lust went out 0 to 12 seconds before the first exposure on every pull that reached one, and nothing is up for the second. A player's own drop is only worth reading against the raid's, which is what the table further down does. Two pulls lived long enough for a third window (pull 11 and pull 12), and that window is where the boss actually falls: short, with nothing left on cooldown, so it reads low and should.

Caustic Waves

170 waves went out; 130 of them clipped somebody, and 15 of those hits killed. Tank contacts are the cost of holding Ula'tek in melee; the other 98 are the avoidable ones.

The boss throws them on two patterns, and they are worth reading apart. Before the split phase it is 2 volleys of about 4 waves, every pull, 96 casts across the night, and they caught non-tanks 26 times — 27% of casts. After it the volleys come about every fifty seconds, up to 4 of them on the longest pulls, about 3 waves each, 74 casts on the nine pulls that got that far, catching non-tanks 72 times, or 97% of casts. Deaths follow the same shape: 2 in the first phase, 13 in the second. No wave has ever landed during the split phase itself.

Left and right

Ten seconds after the first heart closes, the raid splits: two groups, one to each side of the platform, thousands of units apart in the log's coordinates and unmistakable in the position data. The phase opens at 165–165s on every pull and runs 90 to 130 seconds, and the boss cannot be touched until about 110s into it, which is the only deadline that matters for getting back. Across the twelve pulls that reached it the sides were west 9 (1 tank, 2 healers, 6 damage) and east 9 (1 tank, 2 healers, 6 damage). Three players moved to the other side partway through, at pull 2 and pull 7, so each pull is read on its own. No pull had anyone standing on the far side of their own assignment. Deaths out there are listed per player: that is the list to read when deciding who needs an external or a cooldown call.

Defensives in the heavy seconds

The log's own damage curve marks the dangerous parts of a pull: 80 spans across the night where raid damage taken ran at twice the pull's median or more. They are 10% of the time on the boss and 21% of the damage. This is who pressed something in them.

Two bars that don't mean what they look like. The Frost bar sits low because that spec mitigates with a short cooldown rather than a saved one: 194 casts of Ice Barrier, which is not a major by this count. The Blood bar is the opposite, where 169 majors is a rotation rather than 169 saves.

Healthstones & health potions

137 healthstones and 79 health potions went down across 12 pulls, and they went down late rather than early: 93 of the 216 at under 40% health, 20 of those under 20%, 20 above 80%. Average 47%, median 44%. Two of the roster used no healthstone at all; the chart marks them. The potion count is every health potion on the shared cooldown: 37 Concentrated Silvermoon Health Potion, 29 Silvermoon Health Potion and 13 Potent Healing Potion; the stones are 79 Healthstone and 58 Demonic Healthstone.

Early deaths are the 66 deaths that landed more than 15 seconds before the pull's last death, meaning someone dying alone rather than in the wipe. 59 of them had no major defensive in the ten seconds before, and 89 deaths happened in pulls where that player never used a stone or a potion.

Heavy spans are computed, not hand-picked: raid damage taken binned per second, and every run of three or more seconds at twice the pull's median rate or above. Necrotic Vapors, Blight Vein and Acidic Expulsion dominate them. Majors are personal cooldowns (Anti-Magic Shell, Aspect of the Turtle, Astral Shift, Barkskin, Blur, Dark Pact, Desperate Prayer, Divine Protection, Divine Shield, Exhilaration, Greater Invisibility, Ice Cold, Icebound Fortitude, Metamorphosis, Rallying Cry, Shield Wall, Shield of Vengeance, Survival of the Fittest, Unending Resolve, Vampiric Blood). Minor covers the spammables: Blazing Barrier, Dancing Rune Weapon, Expel Harm, Feint, Frenzied Regeneration, Ice Barrier, Prismatic Barrier. Stones and potions are counted from the cast, not the heal, so an overheal still counts as a use. Health at use is the value before the heal landed: WCL stamps the health on both the cast and the heal event as the figure after healing (casts that read 100% health while reporting real, non-overhealed healing give it away), so the effective heal is subtracted back off. Externals are the ones cast on somebody else: Anti-Magic Zone, Aura Mastery, Blessing of Protection, Blessing of Sacrifice, Darkness, Guardian Spirit, Ironbark, Lay on Hands, Spirit Link Totem.

How this was counted

Burn damage is every damage event inside an exposure window landing on either the Venomous Heart or Ula'tek herself, absorbs included, with pet and guardian damage credited to the owner. The heart takes 100% increased damage, so a hit parked there is worth two of the same hit on the boss. The “on heart” column is how much of a player's burn went to the target that pays double. Exposure windows were rebuilt by clustering those events and breaking at gaps over five seconds; all 25 came out at 20 seconds, against 25 Rage of the Shackled casts minus the 0 pulls that died before the first exposure.

Wave hits are damage events from Caustic Waves (spell 1292211, 1292403, 1298570, 1311807 and 1319282), grouped per player when they land within two seconds of each other, so one wave passing through a player counts once. Wave casts count the boss's Caustic Waves casts. A wave death is a death whose killing blow is the wave itself. Damage taken and then dying to something else a second later is not counted here. Phase one is everything before the split phase and phase two everything after it, the split phase being dated from the coordinates as described below; casts within ten seconds of each other count as one volley.

Viper counts are distinct Blightscale Viper instances that took damage in a pull, a proxy for eggs hatched wrong; on this night they track the early pulls more than the deep ones.
