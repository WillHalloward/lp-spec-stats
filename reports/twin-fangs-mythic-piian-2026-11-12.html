The night in short

  • Progress. Best pull 14.4% on pull 28 (Vexhul 7%, Ithraz 22%). On the pulls that reached Submerge, Ithraz ended a median 16 points higher than Vexhul. Median boss health at the wipe went from 61.7% in pulls 1–16 to 39.8% in pulls 17–32. None of the 4 later pulls got the bosses below 15.6%; 1 of them died before 125s, each to a different early mechanic.
  • Against 8 November. Best pull 35.2% → 14.4%. Wipes started by a missed kick: 3 of 26 → 8 of 32. Wipes started by a Tainted Burst: 0 → 1. Visceral Bursts per Rouse: 0.30 → 0.21. Reached Submerge: 13 of 26 → 18 of 32. Median time to the wipe: 138s → 202s.
  • Deaths feed the wipe. 7 wipes began with a globule death (3 soaking one, 4 an explosion); in 6 of them another player had died in the 15 seconds before, and the globules a dead player drops have to be soaked too. Vile Flood killed 3 players before a wipe, in 3 pulls.
  • Dead players’ globules. 1 explosion before a wipe came from a dead player’s globules nobody soaked, about 12 seconds after the death, and 10 deaths came within 1.5 seconds of them. Someone has to pick those up within about ten seconds.
  • Feast. In 4 of the 5 Feast wipes a smash went out to the whole raid because too few were in it. A smash went to the raid or a Tainted Burst went off in 0 of 30 first Feasts, 2 of 21 second, 2 of 14 third and 1 of 4 fourth.
  • Kicks. 17 Broodlings went off before a wipe. Bursts per Rouse: 0.28 in pulls 1–16, 0.15 after. In the second Rouse at a boss position, 10 of 13 were the 4th or 5th Broodling on a side, the ones that can land on a used spot: a job to call for the backups. Commonest reason: no kick, kick was ready (6 of 17). 3 kicks landed just before the Broodling began casting and interrupted nothing.
  • Double soaks. 11 of the 21 globule-soak deaths before a wipe were a second globule within 3 seconds of a first; 2 wipes started with one.

The night on one clock

Every pull of Mythic Twin Fangs runs to the same script, so every pull can share one time axis, running to the length of the longest pull (375 seconds). A pull's row ends where it ended. The wipe starts at the first death of the first run of 4 deaths within 8 seconds. Deaths from there on are drawn small and faded and are not held against anyone. Pick a pull to see it player by player, with soaks, kicks and Feast hits.

Where the pulls broke: before the first Rouse (pull 19), the first Rouse (pull 7), the second Rouse (pulls 1, 4, 5, 9, 11, 12, 20, 27 and 32), the second Feast (pulls 8, 23 and 24) and after Submerge (pulls 2, 3, 6, 10, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 21, 22, 25, 26, 28, 29, 30 and 31). What started the wipes, by the first death of each: Visceral Burst from a missed kick (8), Eternal Venom ticks (5), Ravenous Feast (5), Caustic Globule explosion (4), Caustic Globule soak (3), tank damage (1), Stir the Depths waves (1), other causes (1), Vile Flood (1), Tainted Burst from an unhealed fount (1), Stone Breaker (1) and Coiling Ichor (1). 30 pulls reached the second Rouse.

How the fight runs

Timings are from this log. Both bosses are fought together, and Eternal Venom stacks tie the mechanics together.

Blood Torrent & globules (9s, 70s)Vexhul's Caustic Deluge drops 15 Caustic Globules over five seconds, and Ithraz's spilled blood wraps most of them (11 to 15 a wave this night) in a Barbed Bulwark. Crowd control strips the shield, and one player walks into each globule for one Eternal Venom stack. An unsoaked globule explodes and gives everybody a stack.
Rouse the Brood (36s, 97s)Ten Broodlings surface one by one, five per side, on ten fixed spots in an arc, and start casting Visceral Burst. In the second Rouse at a boss position a spot can be used twice. A kick sends one back into the water. A completed Burst hits the whole raid hard and leaves a stacking DoT.
Stone Breaker (24s, 86s)Ithraz's Stone Breaker lands three times in about seven seconds. It killed 4 players before or at the start of a wipe and started 1 wipe.
Stir the Depths (47s, 108s)Waves cross the room; every one that catches a player gives a stack of Eternal Venom. Waves were 9% of the night’s stacks.
Venomous Emergence (with Rouse)Three Spawn of Vexhul appear and give everyone a stack of Eternal Venom. Each one keeps casting Corrosive Spit lines that add stacks until it dies.
Ravenous Feast (61s, 122s)Three smashes, each split by at least four soakers. Every soak removes one Eternal Venom stack, and a player cannot soak two smashes of one Feast. A smash with too few in it goes out to the whole raid. Blood founts follow and have to be healed into within eight seconds, or Tainted Burst goes off on the raid.
Eternal VenomA permanent stacking DoT. Its ticks grow with the stacks, and guides put the one-shot at nine stacks on Mythic. A player who dies drops up to three globules that have to be soaked, so one death feeds the next.
Submerge (140s)Sanguine Storm, then Vexhul's Vile Flood beam sweeps the room. Every beam tick is a stack. 18 pulls reached it before the wipe.
After SubmergeThe bosses surface in a corner and the cycle starts again: globules at 164s, Rouse at 191s and Feast at 216s, read off pull 31. The Rouse spots move with the bosses.

Rouse the Brood: who kicked what

87 Rouses before a wipe, with 858 Broodlings. 17 Broodlings got a Visceral Burst off (18 Bursts in all, since a Broodling nobody kicks keeps casting), in 16 of the 87 Rouses; the other 71 had none. Nothing after a wipe started is counted. Half of the 17 went off 11.5 seconds or more after the Rouse began. 13 of the 17 came in a second Rouse at a boss position (Rouse 2 or 4). Broodlings went off most often at R5 (3), L4 (3) and L5 (3).

Fixed spots. A Broodling is placed at the nearest of the ten spots by where it first took damage. 32 were kicked before anything hit them, so the log has no position for them. Sides always alternate, so their side is known. 16 of them could also be placed, because only one spot fits the spawn rules (the first Rouse at each boss position, before Submerge and again in the corner after it, uses every spot once). The other 16 are known by side only. Kicking the same spot most of the night (60% or more): Piiaan (L2, 65%), Шамшыммыч (L2, 67%) and Emberforge (R2, 73%).

Was a kick available? For each Broodling that went off, the table at the end of this section lists every kicker who was alive with their interrupt off cooldown. “On that side” means a kicker who normally kicks the side the Broodling was on. For all 17, at least one such kicker was ready. The 9 Broodlings that started a wipe: R3 on pull 1 (Alfi and Indris’s pair), R5 on pull 4 (Laradendusk and Vesemirr’s pair), L4 on pull 5 (Mattiseppo and Aelyssar’s pair), L1 on pull 7 (Piiaan and Niydra’s pair), L4 on pull 10 (Mattiseppo and Aelyssar’s pair), L5 on pull 12 (Laradendusk and Vesemirr’s pair), L2 on pull 20 (Piiaan and Niydra’s pair), L5 on pull 32 (Laradendusk and Vesemirr’s pair) and R5 on pull 32 (Laradendusk and Vesemirr’s pair). The log has no range check, so “ready” means the cooldown was up, not that the Broodling was in reach.

One row per Rouse, spots left to right as in the room. A cell is who kicked that Broodling. The small number after the name is the kick order in that Rouse, so Vesemirr 4 means Vesemirr kicked the 4th Broodling to be kicked; it is not seconds. Red cells went off. After the ✗ is when the first kick aimed at that Broodling came, measured from the Burst: ✗ +0.03s missed by a hair, ✗ +2.5s was sent after the Burst was seen and landed on its next cast. A name after the ✗ means someone else tried first. ✗ no kick: nobody aimed one at it. Dotted underline: there is no position in the log, so the spot is the only one the spawn rules allow. “?” holds the ones even the rules cannot place, with their side (L or R). wipe after a row: a missed kick in that Rouse started the wipe.

Where the second Rouse doubles up. The first Rouse at each boss position puts one Broodling on every spot; the second can reuse one, but only from three or more Broodlings back on that side, so only the 4th and 5th on a side ever land on a used spot. Across 80 Mythic kills a side only ever runs one of the five sequences below (see Rouse spawn pattern). This night 45 of the 58 sides read in full had a repeat. 16 other sides could not be read in full: 12 had a Broodling with no spot and 4 were cut short by the wipe. Tags read pull and side: P3 L is the left side of pull 3’s second Rouse.

The kicker judged is the one the plan gave the Broodling to (early, late or backup by spawn order in the pair; the tanks as one). Kicker dead: dead or out of the pull when it surfaced or went off; the backup’s part is in the details. Kicked before it began casting: their interrupt hit it a moment before the cast started, so it interrupted nothing. Kicked pair-mate’s / another group’s Broodling, double kick (someone had kicked that one already, or within a second), into the boss / a snake: where their interrupt went when it was on cooldown at the Burst. Ravenous Feast and the founts

Soaks. 69 Feasts before a wipe. 4 smashes went out to the whole raid because too few were in them. 4 of the 5 Feasts that started a wipe had a smash go out to the raid (players hit per smash: pull 8 all 17; pull 13 4 · all 16; pull 23 all 18; pull 25 all 18). A smash went to the raid or a Tainted Burst went off in 0 of 30 first Feasts, 2 of 21 second, 2 of 14 third and 1 of 4 fourth. Everyone soaked at least three in four of the Feasts they were alive for. Every soak takes one Eternal Venom stack off, so a skipped Feast is a stack kept. Players who die in a smash that went to the raid are often the ones who did go in; they count in the deaths below.

Globules, shields and Eternal Venom

9 globules exploded unsoaked before a wipe: pulls 5, 8 and 29 in wave 2; pull 2 in wave 3; pulls 18, 21, 22 and 30 in wave 4; pull 24 at 132s from Aelyssar’s globules (10 deaths within 1.5s). The shields were not the problem: 1288 of the 1296 Barbed Bulwarks were stripped by crowd control, mostly by Paladins (263), Shamans (255) and Druids (194). Another 25 went off after a wipe had started, mostly the globules dead players drop: a cost of wiping, not a soak problem.

Each globule is one stack for one player. With about 20 alive and 15 globules a wave, about 5 people go without one in every wave by design, so these lists only mean something when the same name keeps coming up. Tanks are left out. Most important — alive and without a globule in a wave where one exploded: Spânul (6 of 8), Twilightnil (5 of 8), Aeonara (4 of 8) and Шамшыммыч (4 of 8). Soaked in under half the waves they were alive for: nobody. Took two or more in one wave: Huibalast (12 waves), Blossy (9 waves), Alfi (8 waves), Mattiseppo (8 waves) and Twilightnil (8 waves); usually someone covering a gap, at the cost of the extra stack.

The count. 91 globule waves came before a wipe. Each dropped 15 globules. Players who died during a wave dropped 41 more, one to three each, two seconds after the death (Laradendusk on pull 2; Vesemirr on pull 14; Emberforge on pull 16; Vesemirr and Ghani on pull 18; Aelyssar on pull 21; Huibalast on pull 22; Vesemirr on pull 24; Alfi on pull 25; Emberforge and Atheniás on pull 28; Indris and Ghani on pull 28; Vokster on pull 29; Emberforge on pull 31; Vokster on pull 31; Aelyssar on pull 31; Vesemirr on pull 32). Another 138 dropped outside those waves: 132 from players dying away from a wave and 6 not tied to a death. That makes 1544 in all.

Where the stacks came from. 4227 stacks before a wipe: 40% from Venomous Emergence, which everyone takes, 35% from globule soaks, and 4% from globules exploding. 9% came from waves, 7% from snake spit on its target and 1% from spit cleaving someone next to the target, and Vile Flood (2%) and globules landing on someone as they dropped (2%) gave the rest. Most wave and spit-cleave stacks, from not dodging during mechanics: Valanarr (46), Vesemirr (44), Laradendusk (42) and Blossy (28). (The spit’s target can’t dodge the beam, so their stacks are left out.) Most Vile Flood stacks, from standing in the beam during Submerge: Vesemirr (12), Atheniás (10), Aelyssar (9), Ghani (9) and Spânul (9). 18 deaths to Eternal Venom ticks before a wipe started: 1 at 10 stacks, 13 at 9 stacks, 1 at 6 stacks and 3 at 4 stacks. (A Venom death that started a wipe is counted with the wipe causes above.)

Waves soaked is waves with at least one globule, not globules, so taking two in a wave doesn’t raise it. Globules per wave is wave globules soaked divided by waves alive; 15 globules over about 20 people is about 0.75 each. Death globules soaked: a dead player’s globules are extra work, not a missed soak, so they are kept out of the other columns. The log doesn’t say which globule a soak was. In a wave where someone died, a player’s 2nd or later soak after the death drop counts as a death globule, up to the number dropped. Outside a wave, any soak 2–15 s after a death counts.

Double soaks. One globule soak takes most of a health bar, so a second one inside 3 seconds, before the healers have caught up, kills. 18 players died that way, 11 of them before a wipe or as its first death (2 wipes started with one). That is 11 of the 21 soak deaths before a wipe; the other 10 died to a single globule. The median gap between the two soaks was 1.1 seconds. Most: Aelyssar (3), Emberforge (3), Ghani (2) and Alfi (2).

The snakes

87 Venomous Emergences before a wipe. All three snakes died in 77 of them, the last at a median of 13.2 seconds after they surfaced. They got off 300 Corrosive Spits, a median of 3 per Emergence. Every spit that lands is a stack: 7% of the night’s stacks were on the spit’s target, and 1% on players caught in the cleave.

Deaths

69 deaths came before a wipe started, and 541 after. Players were dying before most wipes: in 22 of 32 pulls someone died before the wipe started. Pulls that reached Submerge averaged 3.0 deaths before the wipe and the rest 1.1, mostly because they ran longer: per minute it is 0.73 against 0.61. Most deaths before a wipe: Blossy (8), Emberforge (7), Vesemirr (6), Huibalast (5) and Spânul (5). First to die in a pull: Emberforge (5), Blossy (3) and Huibalast (3). A death by Toxic Fumes (the room's constant damage) is counted as the biggest hit in the five seconds before it.

How this is read. A wipe starts at the first death of the first run of 4 deaths within 8 seconds; the kill, if there is one, has none. A mechanic that opens after the wipe started is not counted, and inside one that was already running, nothing after the wipe start counts (Broodlings, Bursts, kicks, smashes, snake damage). A globule wave cut off by the wipe within 7.5 seconds (before its drops end) is not a wave; its globules are counted on their own. What started a wipe is the cause of its first death. Alive: a player is dead from their death until they next cast something, so a battle rez brings them back. A Broodling's spot is the nearest of the ten spots to where it first took damage, within seven yards. A kick ready means the kicker was alive and their interrupt's cooldown was up when the Burst completed; the night's most-used interrupt counts for each player. A globule soak is a Caustic Globule hit on one player. An explosion is the other Caustic Globule spell hitting three or more players within 0.6 seconds. A stack's source is the hit that landed from 0.35 seconds before the stack to 0.15 seconds after it. A shield strip is a Barbed Bulwark dropping within 0.4 seconds of a stun, fear or incapacitate on that globule. Healing into a fount is healing absorbed on a player while they carry Tainted Blood, leaving out anyone also under Blood Torrent's absorb.
