The night on one clock

Every pull of Mythic Twin Fangs runs to the same script, so every pull can share one time axis, capped at the longest pull (272 seconds). A pull's row ends where it ended. The wipe starts at the first death of the first run of 4 deaths within 8 seconds. Deaths from there on are drawn small and faded and are not held against anyone. Pick a pull to see it player by player, with soaks, kicks and Feast hits.

Where the pulls broke: before the first Rouse (pull 6), the first Feast and its founts (pulls 1, 7, 20 and 23), the second Rouse (pulls 3, 9, 11, 12 and 26), the second Feast (pulls 4, 5 and 19) and after Submerge (pulls 2, 8, 10, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 21, 22, 24 and 25). What started the wipes: ravenous feast (10), caustic globule (7), visceral burst (missed kick) (3), stone breaker (3), tainted burst (fount not healed) (1), stir the depths waves (1) and eternal venom stacks (1). 23 pulls reached the second Rouse, the first of them pull 2.

How the fight runs

Read off this log. Both bosses are fought together, and Eternal Venom stacks are what ties the mechanics together.

Blood Torrent & globules (9s, 70s)Vexhul's Caustic Deluge drops 15 Caustic Globules over five seconds, and Ithraz's spilled blood wraps most of them (9 to 15 a wave this night) in a Barbed Bulwark. Crowd control strips the shield, and one player walks into each globule for one Eternal Venom stack. An unsoaked globule explodes and gives everybody a stack.
Rouse the Brood (36s, 97s)Ten Broodlings surface one by one at ten fixed spots in an arc, five per side, and start casting Visceral Burst. A kick sends one back into the water. A completed Burst hits the whole raid hard and leaves a stacking DoT.
Venomous Emergence (with Rouse)Three Spawn of Vexhul appear and give everyone a stack of Eternal Venom. Each one keeps casting Corrosive Spit lines that add stacks until it dies.
Ravenous Feast (61s, 122s)Three smashes, each split by at least four soakers. Every soak removes one Eternal Venom stack, and Feasted stops the same player soaking twice. Blood founts follow and have to be healed into within eight seconds, or Tainted Burst goes off on the raid.
Eternal VenomA permanent stacking DoT. Its ticks grow with the stacks, and guides put the one-shot at nine stacks on Mythic. On Mythic, a player who dies to it drops more globules, so one Venom death feeds the next.
Submerge (140s)Sanguine Storm, then Vexhul's Vile Flood beam sweeps the room. Every beam tick is a stack. 13 pulls reached it.

Rouse the Brood: who kicked what

55 Rouses before a wipe, 550 Broodlings, 545 kicked. 26 Visceral Bursts went off, and 41 Rouses had none. The misses came late: half of the 19 Broodlings that went off did so 11.5 seconds or more into the Rouse, after the first round of kicks was on cooldown. Misses came most often from L3 (4), R1 (4) and L4 (3).

Fixed spots. A Broodling is placed at the nearest of the ten spots by where it first took damage; 43 were kicked before anything hit them and have no spot. Kicking the same spot most of the night (60% or more): Piiaan (L1, 62%), Indris (R3, 60%), Aelyssar (L4, 64%) and Preng (R2, 75%). Kicking one side but no fixed spot: Alfi (right side), Atheniás (right side), Mattiseppo (left side), Taranus (left side), Squashiclysm (right side), Шамшыммыч (left side), Angryde (right side), Arozax (left side) and Balkaneros (right side). Kicking in under 40% of the Rouses they were alive for: Huibalast (18 of 53), Шамшыммыч (16 of 54), Angryde (10 of 52) and Arozax (6 of 51).

A kick ready when it went off. For each Burst, the table lists every kicker who was alive with their interrupt off cooldown. “On that side” means a kicker who normally kicks the side the Broodling was on. For 18 of the 19 missed Broodlings, at least one such kicker was ready. Across the Bursts in Rouses that ended a pull, the kickers most often ready on that side were Balkaneros (7), Arozax (5), Laradendusk (4) and Taranus (4). Where the second Rouse doubles up. The first Rouse in a place puts one Broodling on every spot; the second can reuse one, but only from three or more Broodlings back on that side, so only the 4th and 5th on a side ever land on a used spot. Across 80 Mythic kills a side only ever runs one of the five sequences below (see Rouse spawn pattern). Tonight 19 of the 29 sides read in full had a repeat; 15 more had a Broodling with no spot.

Ravenous Feast and the founts

Soaks. 45 Feasts before a wipe; 6 smashes landed with fewer than four soakers. Everyone soaked: nobody missed every Feast they were alive for (nobody), and only nobody soaked fewer than three in four. Every soak takes one Eternal Venom stack off, so a skipped Feast is a stack kept.

Tainted Blood. 4 of the 45 Feasts ended in Tainted Burst. Healing absorbed by Tainted Blood on the players standing in the founts came from Arúgál (28%), Valanarr (26%), Balkaneros (20%) and Aeonara (17%); everyone else together gave 9%.

Globules, shields and Eternal Venom

60 globule waves came before a wipe. Each dropped 15 globules, and 10 waves dropped a few more (17 on pull 7, 16 on pull 9, 17 on pull 10, 16 on pull 12, 23 on pull 16, 21 on pull 17, 16 on pull 19, 17 on pull 23, 18 on pull 24 and 18 on pull 25), 929 in all. 808 of them were shielded, and 803 of those 808 Barbed Bulwarks were stripped by crowd control, so the shields were never the problem. That job falls to whichever classes bring an area stun or fear, mostly Paladins (176), Druids (164) and Shamans (109). 10 globules exploded unsoaked before a wipe, on pull 3 wave 2, pull 4 wave 1, pull 7 wave 2, pull 8 wave 2, pull 12 wave 2, pull 14 wave 4, pull 19 wave 2, pull 20 wave 2, pull 22 wave 2 and pull 24 wave 2. Another 20 went off after a wipe had started. Those were mostly the globules dead players drop, which is a cost of wiping and not a soak problem.

Each globule is one stack for one player, so nobody should take two in a wave. Took two or more in one wave: Arúgál (9), Aelyssar (9), Alfi (6), Angryde (5) and Valanarr (4). With fewer globules than players, that is usually someone covering a gap, and costs only the extra stack. Soaked in under half the waves they were alive for: Arozax (14 of 57). Alive and without a globule that wave when one exploded: Vesemirr (8 of 10), Arozax (8 of 10), Piiaan (6 of 10), Шамшыммыч (6 of 10), Huibalast (5 of 10) and Atheniás (5 of 9). Of all of these, that last list matters most.

Where the stacks came from. 3087 stacks before a wipe: 35% from Venomous Emergence, which everyone takes, 33% from globule soaks, and 10% from globules exploding. 7% came from snake spit, and the Stir the Depths waves (3), Vile Flood (103) and Caustic Deluge on the tanks (67) gave the rest. 298 stacks (10%) had no hit that could be matched to them. Most spit and wave stacks: Шамшыммыч (19), Arozax (18), Indris (17) and Balkaneros (16). On pull 14, 9 players died to Venom ticks at 7 stacks, before the nine-stack limit. The wipe started at 252.5 seconds.

The snakes

55 Venomous Emergences before a wipe. All three snakes died in 54 of them, the last at a median of 14.4 seconds after they surfaced. They got off 204 Corrosive Spits, 3 at the median per Emergence. Every spit that lands is a stack.

Below is each dps's share of their own damage that went into the snakes while any were alive. Most of it: Preng (90.2%), Atheniás (77.2%), Шамшыммыч (76.0%) and Huibalast (75.5%). Barely touched them: Mattiseppo (8.4%) and Indris (1.2%). The snakes were cleaved down together, not taken one at a time. For nearly everyone, about a third of their snake damage went into the first one to die, which is an even split across the three.

Deaths

58 deaths came before a wipe started, and 453 after. The wipes were sudden: 6 of 26 pulls had nobody dead until the cascade, so these pulls were lost to one mechanic, not to bleeding players. Most deaths before a wipe: Angryde (7), Squashiclysm (7), Preng (5) and Taranus (5). First to die in a pull: Laradendusk (3), Preng (2) and Squashiclysm (2). A death by Toxic Fumes (the room's constant damage) is blamed on the biggest hit in the five seconds before it.

How this is read. A wipe starts at the first death of the first run of 4 deaths within 8 seconds; the kill, if there is one, has none. A mechanic that opens after the wipe started is not counted. Neither is a globule wave cut off by the wipe within ten seconds. A Broodling's spot is the nearest of the ten spots to where it first took damage, within six yards. A kick ready means the kicker was alive and their interrupt's cooldown was up when the Burst completed; the night's most-used interrupt counts for each player. A globule soak is a Caustic Globule hit on one player. An explosion is the other Caustic Globule spell hitting three or more players within 0.6 seconds. A stack's source is the hit that landed within 0.15 seconds of the stack. A shield strip is a Barbed Bulwark dropping within 0.4 seconds of a stun, fear or incapacitate on that globule. Healing into a fount is healing absorbed on a player while they carry Tainted Blood, leaving out anyone also under Blood Torrent's absorb.
