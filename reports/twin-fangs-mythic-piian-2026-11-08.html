The night in short

  • Progress. Best pull 35.2% on pull 14 (Vexhul 24%, Ithraz 47%). On the pulls that reached Submerge, Ithraz ended a median 22 points higher than Vexhul. Median boss health at the wipe went from 64.8% in pulls 1–13 to 48.5% in pulls 14–26. None of the 12 later pulls got the bosses below 43.3%; 4 of them died before 125s, each to a different early mechanic.
  • Against 5 November. Best pull 41.7% → 35.2%. Wipes started by a missed kick: 9 of 20 → 3 of 26. Wipes started by a Tainted Burst: 4 → 0. Visceral Bursts per Rouse: 0.60 → 0.30. Reached Submerge: 1 of 20 → 13 of 26. Median time to the wipe: 73s → 138s.
  • Deaths feed the wipe. 11 wipes began with a globule death (7 soaking one, 4 an explosion); in 8 of them another player had died in the 15 seconds before, and the globules a dead player drops have to be soaked too. Vile Flood killed 6 players before a wipe, in 5 pulls; in 3 of them the wipe followed within 15 seconds.
  • Dead players’ globules. 3 explosions before a wipe came from a dead player’s globules nobody soaked, about 12 seconds after the death, and 25 deaths came within 1.5 seconds of them. Someone has to pick those up within about ten seconds.
  • Feast. In all 4 Feast wipes a smash went out to the whole raid because too few were in it. A smash went to the raid or a Tainted Burst went off in 1 of 25 first Feasts, 2 of 16 second and 1 of 4 third.
  • Kicks. 16 Broodlings went off before a wipe. Bursts per Rouse: 0.42 in pulls 1–13, 0.20 after. In the second Rouse at a boss position, 7 of 12 were the 4th or 5th Broodling on a side, the ones that can land on a used spot: a job to call for the backups.
  • Globules. 8 of the 10 explosions inside a wave came in the second wave (Blood Torrent at about 70s): 4 in pulls 1–13 and 4 after.

The night on one clock

Every pull of Mythic Twin Fangs runs to the same script, so every pull can share one time axis, running to the length of the longest pull (272 seconds). A pull's row ends where it ended. The wipe starts at the first death of the first run of 4 deaths within 8 seconds. Deaths from there on are drawn small and faded and are not held against anyone. Pick a pull to see it player by player, with soaks, kicks and Feast hits.

Where the pulls broke: before the first Rouse (pull 6), the first Feast and its founts (pulls 1, 7, 20 and 23), the second Rouse (pulls 3, 9, 11, 12 and 26), the second Feast (pulls 4, 5 and 19) and after Submerge (pulls 2, 8, 10, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 21, 22, 24 and 25). What started the wipes, by the first death of each: Caustic Globule soak (7), Ravenous Feast (4), Caustic Globule explosion (4), Visceral Burst from a missed kick (3), Eternal Venom ticks (3), Stone Breaker (2), attrition from Toxic Fumes (2) and Stir the Depths waves (1). 21 pulls reached the second Rouse.

How the fight runs

Timings are from this log. Both bosses are fought together, and Eternal Venom stacks tie the mechanics together.

Blood Torrent & globules (9s, 70s)Vexhul's Caustic Deluge drops 15 Caustic Globules over five seconds, and Ithraz's spilled blood wraps most of them (9 to 15 a wave this night) in a Barbed Bulwark. Crowd control strips the shield, and one player walks into each globule for one Eternal Venom stack. An unsoaked globule explodes and gives everybody a stack.
Rouse the Brood (36s, 97s)Ten Broodlings surface one by one, five per side, on ten fixed spots in an arc, and start casting Visceral Burst. In the second Rouse at a boss position a spot can be used twice. A kick sends one back into the water. A completed Burst hits the whole raid hard and leaves a stacking DoT.
Stone Breaker (24s, 86s)Ithraz's Stone Breaker lands three times in about seven seconds. It killed 19 players before or at the start of a wipe and started 2 wipes.
Stir the Depths (47s, 108s)Waves cross the room; every one that catches a player gives a stack of Eternal Venom. Waves were 10% of the night’s stacks.
Venomous Emergence (with Rouse)Three Spawn of Vexhul appear and give everyone a stack of Eternal Venom. Each one keeps casting Corrosive Spit lines that add stacks until it dies.
Ravenous Feast (61s, 122s)Three smashes, each split by at least four soakers. Every soak removes one Eternal Venom stack, and a player cannot soak two smashes of one Feast. A smash with too few in it goes out to the whole raid. Blood founts follow and have to be healed into within eight seconds, or Tainted Burst goes off on the raid.
Eternal VenomA permanent stacking DoT. Its ticks grow with the stacks, and guides put the one-shot at nine stacks on Mythic. A player who dies drops up to three globules that have to be soaked, so one death feeds the next.
Submerge (140s)Sanguine Storm, then Vexhul's Vile Flood beam sweeps the room. Every beam tick is a stack. 13 pulls reached it before the wipe.
After SubmergeThe bosses surface in a corner and the cycle starts again: globules at 164s, Rouse at 191s and Feast at 216s, read off pull 14. The Rouse spots move with the bosses.

Rouse the Brood: who kicked what

54 Rouses before a wipe, with 534 Broodlings. 16 Broodlings got a Visceral Burst off, in 13 of the 54 Rouses; the other 41 had none. Nothing after a wipe started is counted. Half of the 16 went off 10.0 seconds or more after the Rouse began. 12 of the 16 came in a second Rouse at a boss position (Rouse 2). Broodlings went off most often at L3 (3), L4 (3), R1 (3) and L5 (3).

Fixed spots. A Broodling is placed at the nearest of the ten spots by where it first took damage. 42 were kicked before anything hit them, so the log has no position for them. Sides always alternate, so their side is known. 23 of them could also be placed, because only one spot fits the spawn rules (the first Rouse at each boss position, before Submerge and again in the corner after it, uses every spot once). The other 19 are known by side only. Kicking the same spot most of the night (60% or more): Piiaan (L1, 63%), Indris (R3, 61%) and Aelyssar (L4, 63%).

Was a kick available? For each Broodling that went off, the table at the end of this section lists every kicker who was alive with their interrupt off cooldown. “On that side” means a kicker who normally kicks the side the Broodling was on. For all 16, at least one such kicker was ready. The 5 Broodlings that started a wipe: R2 on pull 3 (Atheniás and Preng’s pair), L4 on pull 3 (Mattiseppo and Aelyssar’s pair), R2 on pull 9 (Atheniás and Preng’s pair), R1 on pull 9 (Atheniás and Preng’s pair) and R5 on pull 25 (Laradendusk and Vesemirr’s pair). The log has no range check, so “ready” means the cooldown was up, not that the Broodling was in reach.

Where the second Rouse doubles up. The first Rouse at each boss position puts one Broodling on every spot; the second can reuse one, but only from three or more Broodlings back on that side, so only the 4th and 5th on a side ever land on a used spot. Across 80 Mythic kills a side only ever runs one of the five sequences below (see Rouse spawn pattern). This night 19 of the 27 sides read in full had a repeat. 15 other sides could not be read in full: 14 had a Broodling with no spot and 1 was cut short by the wipe. Tags read pull and side: P3 L is the left side of pull 3’s second Rouse.

Ravenous Feast and the founts

Soaks. 45 Feasts before a wipe. 4 smashes went out to the whole raid because too few were in them. All 4 Feasts that started a wipe had a smash go out to the raid (players hit per smash: pull 1 8 · 7 · all 20; pull 4 9 · 5 · all 20; pull 17 8 · all 18; pull 19 all 18). In 2 of them the other smashes had 12 or more between them, so the people were there but not spread across all three. A smash went to the raid or a Tainted Burst went off in 1 of 25 first Feasts, 2 of 16 second and 1 of 4 third. Everyone soaked at least three in four of the Feasts they were alive for. Alfi took 69 globule-soak stacks and skipped 11 of 44 Feasts; Feast soaks are the only way to shed stacks. Every soak takes one Eternal Venom stack off, so a skipped Feast is a stack kept. Players who die in a smash that went to the raid are often the ones who did go in; they count in the deaths below.

Tainted Blood. 0 of the 45 Feasts ended in Tainted Burst. Healing into the founts came from Arúgál (29%), Valanarr (26%), Balkaneros (20%) and Aeonara (17%); everyone else together gave 8%.

Globules, shields and Eternal Venom

13 globules exploded unsoaked before a wipe: pull 4 in wave 1; pulls 3, 7, 8, 12, 19, 20, 22 and 24 in wave 2; pull 14 in wave 4; pull 5 at 123s from Angryde’s globules (1 death within 1.5s), pull 13 at 165s from Alfi’s globules (12 deaths within 1.5s) and pull 18 at 164s from Squashiclysm’s globules (12 deaths within 1.5s). The shields were not the problem: 817 of the 822 Barbed Bulwarks were stripped by crowd control, mostly by Paladins (177), Druids (166) and Shamans (109). Another 20 went off after a wipe had started, mostly the globules dead players drop: a cost of wiping, not a soak problem.

Each globule is one stack for one player. With about 20 alive and 15 globules a wave, about 5 people go without one in every wave by design, so these lists only mean something when the same name keeps coming up. Tanks are left out. Most important — alive and without a globule in a wave where one exploded: Arozax (8 of 10), Piiaan (6 of 10), Шамшыммыч (6 of 10), Atheniás (5 of 9), Huibalast (5 of 10) and Aeonara (4 of 10). Soaked in under half the waves they were alive for: Arozax (14 of 59). Took two or more in one wave: Aelyssar (9 waves), Arúgál (9 waves), Alfi (6 waves), Angryde (6 waves) and Squashiclysm (5 waves); usually someone covering a gap, at the cost of the extra stack.

The count. 61 globule waves came before a wipe. Each dropped 15 globules. Players who died during a wave dropped 27 more, one to three each, two seconds after the death (Arozax on pull 9; Vesemirr on pull 10; Taranus on pull 12; Taranus, Alfi and Piiaan on pull 16; Angryde and Squashiclysm on pull 17; Atheniás on pull 19; Angryde on pull 23; Piiaan on pull 24; Angryde on pull 25). Another 118 dropped outside those waves: 113 from players dying away from a wave and 5 not tied to a death. That makes 1060 in all.

Where the stacks came from. 3087 stacks before a wipe: 35% from Venomous Emergence, which everyone takes, 32% from globule soaks, and 10% from globules exploding. 10% came from Stir the Depths waves and 7% from snake spit, and Vile Flood (3%) and globules landing on someone as they dropped (2%) gave the rest. 4 stacks had nothing that could be matched to them. Most spit, wave and Vile Flood stacks: Valanarr (45), Arozax (43), Angryde (42) and Laradendusk (42). 11 deaths to Eternal Venom ticks before a wipe started: 7 at 9 stacks, 1 at 7 stacks, 1 at 6 stacks and 2 at 4 stacks. (A Venom death that started a wipe is counted with the wipe causes above.)

Deaths

58 deaths came before a wipe started, and 453 after. Players were dying before most wipes: in 20 of 26 pulls someone died before the wipe started. Pulls that reached Submerge averaged 3.4 deaths before the wipe and the rest 1.1, mostly because they ran longer: per minute it is 1.02 against 0.65. Most deaths before a wipe: Angryde (7), Squashiclysm (7), Preng (5) and Taranus (5). First to die in a pull: Laradendusk (3), Angryde (2), Atheniás (2), Preng (2), Squashiclysm (2) and Taranus (2). A death by Toxic Fumes (the room's constant damage) is counted as the biggest hit in the five seconds before it.

How this is read. A wipe starts at the first death of the first run of 4 deaths within 8 seconds; the kill, if there is one, has none. A mechanic that opens after the wipe started is not counted, and inside one that was already running, nothing after the wipe start counts (Broodlings, Bursts, kicks, smashes, snake damage). A globule wave cut off by the wipe within 7.5 seconds (before its drops end) is not a wave; its globules are counted on their own. What started a wipe is the cause of its first death. Alive: a player is dead from their death until they next cast something, so a battle rez brings them back. A Broodling's spot is the nearest of the ten spots to where it first took damage, within seven yards. A kick ready means the kicker was alive and their interrupt's cooldown was up when the Burst completed; the night's most-used interrupt counts for each player. A globule soak is a Caustic Globule hit on one player. An explosion is the other Caustic Globule spell hitting three or more players within 0.6 seconds. A stack's source is the hit that landed within 0.15 seconds of the stack. A shield strip is a Barbed Bulwark dropping within 0.4 seconds of a stun, fear or incapacitate on that globule. Healing into a fount is healing absorbed on a player while they carry Tainted Blood, leaving out anyone also under Blood Torrent's absorb.
