How this was counted

Burn damage is every damage event inside an exposure window landing on either the Venomous Heart or Ula'tek herself, absorbs included, with pet and guardian damage credited to the owner. The heart takes 100% increased damage, so a hit parked there is worth two of the same hit on the boss — the “on heart” column is how much of a player's burn went to the target that pays double. Exposure windows were rebuilt by clustering those events and breaking at gaps over five seconds; all {{n_windows}} came out at {{window_len}} seconds, against {{expose_casts}} {{expose_name}} casts minus the {{no_window_pulls_word}} pulls that died before the first exposure.

Wave hits are damage events from {{wave_name}} (spell {{wave_dmg_ids}}), grouped per player when they land within two seconds of each other, so one wave passing through a player counts once. Wave casts count the boss's {{wave_name}} casts. A wave death is a death whose killing blow is the wave itself — damage taken and then dying to something else a second later is not counted here.

Viper counts are distinct Blightscale Viper instances that took damage in a pull, a proxy for eggs hatched wrong; on this night they track the early pulls more than the deep ones.
