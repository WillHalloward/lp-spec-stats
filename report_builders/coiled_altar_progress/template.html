Every pull

One bar per pull, a row per night, in the order they were pulled. Height is how long the pull lived before its wipe started; colour is the furthest stage it reached. Quick resets are left out. Hover for what ended it.

How far the pulls got

The share of each night’s pulls still alive past each point of the fight, before its wipe started: past the opening orbs, past each Guillotine of stage one (48s and 133s), into stage two, through the first shield, into the intermission and into stage three. Where a night’s bar drops sharply is the wall that night. Quick resets are left out.

Night by night

Every number is a rate where the size of the night would otherwise decide it: per pull, per minute of the stage played before the wipe, or per window. The small arrow is the change from the night before, green where that is better and red where it is worse. Only what happened before each wipe counts.

What ended the pulls

Each wipe’s cause, as on the night pages: what killed the raid in the first eight seconds after the wipe started, with a purple explosion or a missed Guillotine named outright when it did most of that killing, and called reset for a wipe that opens with the raid jumping off. Shares are of that night’s wipes.

Deaths before the wipe

Every death before its pull’s wipe started, by mechanic, per ten pulls so the nights compare. Darker is more. Falls with no killing blow are split into Dreadmarch, ghost catch, the handover knockback and a plain fall.

Who set off the purple explosions

An orb touching a purple drops its carrier’s orb debuff on the explosion’s own millisecond, so whoever loses one then set it off; in a collision both carriers do. Nobody’s orb came off is most likely two orbs meeting on the ground, though a purple carrier was sometimes close by and kept theirs; those are left unattributed. Explosions before each wipe only; the night pages list every one with how long the orb was held and how far each carrier moved in the second before.

Players

One mechanic at a time, player by player, night by night. A blank is a night they were not in, or had nothing to count. Hover a cell for the raw numbers.
