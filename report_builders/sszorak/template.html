How Mythic Sszorak runs

Read off this log rather than a guide: every pull ran to the same clock. Each phase one has two Apex Predator combos, and each combo is followed by a Serpent's Fury pop, a Venomous Surge and a Crosswinds set. Dig In then opens a 25-second intermission, and the cycle repeats.

Apex PredatorFive tank casts in a random order: two Ravage, two Mutilate, one Tempest. Ravage is a frontal on the tank; anyone else in it takes the hit ({{ravage_raid}} times this night, killing {{ravage_dead}}). Mutilate is a frontal the raid splits into two soak groups.
TempestTornadoes come out of the boss and roam the room for most of the next minute. {{tor_first10_pct}}% of the night's tornado hits came in the first ten seconds after the cast and {{tor_late_pct}}% more than fifteen seconds after it, while the raid was busy with Surge and Crosswinds.
Serpent's FuryOne player is marked. Sszorak builds rage until fourteen more stand in the circle with them, {{fury_need}} in all, a hard minimum; then he charges the mark (To the Slaughter) and everyone in it gets Virulence, which bursts in lines five seconds later. At 100 rage he gains Unbound Ferocity, and the pull is over.
Venomous Surge & Viscous CystTwo players are marked for ten seconds and drop a cyst where they stand. Touching a cyst pops it: a big knockback and a damage-over-time on everyone near it. The cysts are for the intermission.
Raging CrosswindsEight players get an arrow and are launched along it about eight seconds later. Two players with opposite arrows who meet cancel each other. Every class except {{xw_partner_classes}} can also cancel their own launch with a movement ability.
Dig InThe intermission. Winds sweep the platform in three gusts; the raid pops a cyst before each one at about {{pop1}}, {{pop2}} and {{pop3}} seconds in, so the knockback carries everyone into the wind instead of off the edge.

The Serpent's Fury clock

The pop needs {{fury_need}} bodies in the circle, the marked player included, so a raid with {{fury_need_less}} alive and no battle res ready cannot pop at all. {{enrage_all_n}} pulls reached Unbound Ferocity. In {{enrage_forced_n}} of them ({{enrage_forced_list}}) the raid was already under {{fury_need}} when it came, so the deaths ended those pulls, not the clock. The other {{enrage_n}} ({{enrage_list}}) still had the numbers, and the pop came too late. Every pop that did go off had {{fury_need}} in the circle: {{soak_med}} players at the median by Virulence and never fewer than {{soak_lo}}. On the two pops with only fourteen players, the marked player was Piiaan, whose Beast Mastery pet stands in but never gets the debuff.

The boss reaches 100 rage {{fury_short}} seconds after a pop within a phase, and {{fury_long}} seconds after the last pop before an intermission, because the rage keeps building through Dig In. The first pop after the intermission cannot go off until the post-intermission combo ends; when it worked it went off at {{pop3_med}} seconds at the median. So the second pop of the pull sets the deadline for the third. Pop two landed anywhere from {{pop2_lo}} to {{pop2_hi}} seconds this night, and every second earlier is a second less after the combo.

Of the timing failures, {{enrage_slot3}} pulls died at that first post-intermission pop ({{enrage_slot3_list}}). {{enrage_slot4}} died at the next one ({{enrage_slot4_list}}), and {{enrage_slot5}} after the second intermission ({{enrage_slot5_list}}): {{p5_rows}}.

Against the plan (every dps and both tanks in, healers only to cover a dead one), {{fury_full}} of {{fury_pops}} pops had every alive dps and tank in the circle. Missing most often while alive: {{fury_missers}}. Healers stood in when no dps or tank was dead on {{fury_extra_pops}} pops: {{fury_extra_names}}. A healer who soaks takes a Virulence they did not need, which is one more set of lines in the raid. Per player, before each pull was over. Fury missed: pops where they were alive, assigned (dps or tank) and not in the circle. Fury extra: healers standing in when no dps or tank was dead. Clipped: Virulence picked up from somebody else's lines.

How this is read. The platform's edge is about 60 yards from the middle. No player still alive fifteen seconds later was ever logged past 69. A death whose last eight seconds went past 65 yards, with no killing blow, is a fall. It is blamed on what moved the player there in the seven seconds before they left the platform: a Crosswinds launch, a cyst pop, the intermission's winds, or nothing. Nothing means they walked or jumped off. Three of those inside ten seconds is a called wipe. Under the number is fewer than {{fury_need}} alive, counting a ready battle res as one more. Charges follow the raid rule: one at the pull and one every 4.5 minutes with twenty players, spent by Rebirth or Intercession; a shaman's Reincarnation is free. Every death more than three seconds after either point is left out. A tornado hit is a Tempest hit without the tick flag. A cyst pop is a burst of Viscous Cyst hits without the tick flag; pops during Dig In or in the ten seconds after it are planned, all others are accidents. A Fury pop is the mark dropping with Virulence going out to the soakers within 1.5 seconds; the rage windows are read off the enrages themselves. A Crosswinds launch is Turbulent Gusts lasting past 1.5 seconds. A solo cancel is the player's own movement ability ({{solo_moves}}) cast from half a second before to 1.5 seconds after the launch. A launch with that movement pressed later counts as late.
