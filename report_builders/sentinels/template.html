The puzzle

Every player gets Helical Toxins with one to three orbs. Two players whose orbs make exactly four clear each other on touch; any other touch merges their stacks without clearing, and spends orbs somebody else needed. A 1 and a 1 become two 2s, a 1 and a 2 two 3s, so every stack increase is a wrong pairing even when it stays under four. Stasis does not wait for the puzzle: the debuff lasts about 28 seconds, runs on after Stasis ends, and whoever still holds it when it runs out takes Cultivated Burst. So a solve is every player clearing before the debuff expires, and the solve time is when the last of them did.

Of the night's {{pz_n}} intermissions, {{pz_solved}} were solved and {{pz_failed}} failed on a wrong pairing ({{pz_wrong_list}}). The other {{pz_broken}} were lost to deaths or a called wipe: {{pz_broken_down}} opened with more than two of the raid already dead, and the rest had a player die holding the debuff, never to be resolved, before anyone paired wrong. A corpse keeps its counter and can still be bumped and cleared, so a death only counts here when the debuff was never resolved. {{pz_broken_called}} of those pulls were over within twenty seconds of the debuff running out. Counting only the {{pz_decided}} the puzzle decided, {{pz_fail_pct}}% failed. {{kill_pz_note}}

Wrong pairings are counted two ways. One broke the puzzle when it was the first in an intermission that could still be solved: two or fewer dead going in, and no unresolved death before it. Every other merge is counted as after it was lost. That covers a called wipe, where pairing wrong on purpose ends Stasis so the bosses can be kited out and reset, and a puzzle cascading on its own. The log cannot tell those two apart, so neither is held against anyone. {{pz_after_total}} merges across the night fall in that second group.

How this was counted

Intermissions are the Vitriolic Stasis buff on the bosses, start to end. The heal is every Vitriolic Stasis healing event on either boss inside one. Boss health is read off the bosses' own casts, which carry their hit points; a cast lands every few seconds per boss, so the race chart is a few seconds coarse, but the heal is exact.

Teams come from the marks. Each player's side in a phase is the mark they collected most of in it, counted only when three or more stacks and 70% of their applications agree, so the walk across does not count. The two teams are anchored on the players who share a side most often across the night, which fixes which side each team stood on in every phase. Each player is then placed with a team pull by pull, when three in four of their readable phases agree; a change that holds for three pulls or more counts as a move. A player who cannot be placed in a pull, standing between the bosses or collecting both marks, is counted on their own for that pull. Boss damage comes from the damage table, phase by phase, which rolls pets into their owners. Cross-room damage is a team member's damage onto the boss the other team was standing on in that phase.

The puzzle is read over the Helical Toxins debuff's own 28 seconds, not Stasis's. A clear is two removals within 150 ms before it runs out, which holds for a corpse being bumped as much as for two living players. A wrong pairing is any stack increase, and an intermission is solved when every player, dead or alive, cleared. A failed one is put down to deaths or a called wipe when it opened with more than two dead, or when a player died holding the debuff, never resolved, before the first wrong pairing. Missed droplets are clusters of Noxious Blast hits within 100 ms, matched to the Toxic Droplets cast before them. Protovenom pairs are two marked players' debuffs dropping together; an eruption is a cluster of Protovenom Eruption hits, its carrier the marked player among the targets.
