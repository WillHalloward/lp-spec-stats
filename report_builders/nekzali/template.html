The arithmetic

What a dive costs, and when the fight can afford it

Grasping Depths wakes a Drowned Echo in the Soulcoil Well and drags the raid towards it, ticking shadow damage on all twenty players until the Echo is dead. Killing it means sending people down. Climbing back out applies Soul Exhaustion, which for {{exhaustion}} seconds adds 300% to anything the well does to you, so the same five cannot simply go again.

That makes a dive cost {{dive_cost}} seconds rather than {{dive_len}}: about {{dive_len}} in the water until the Echo dies, then {{exhaustion}} locked out. Whether two teams can cover that depends entirely on how often the Echo comes back, and the fight answers that differently in each stage. All three bars are on the same scale.

How the kills do it

Four day-one kills, none of them on three teams

The earliest public Mythic kills on record, all four from 2 September, the first day the difficulty opened. Every one of them ran two teams, the same as this raid does. Between them they took no Soul Exhaustion penalties at all.

Two teams works for them because a {{kill_dur}}-second kill only ever asks for six dives. Their cadence compresses the same way this one does, but the {{cadence}}-second regime only lasts for the last two Echoes, and two teams covers exactly two. Then the boss dies.

Three of the four never dive the final Echo at all. It wakes around 397s, they leave it alive, eat Grasping Depths for seventeen to twenty seconds, and kill the boss through it. The last Echo is something they outrun rather than solve.

The call

What to change before next week

The obvious fix is a third well team. The kill logs say that is the wrong first move: nobody who killed this on day one used three, and this rotation's shape already matches theirs. In priority order:

Don't dive the last Echo

Free, and available next pull. Three of the four kills leave the final Echo alive and burn through Grasping Depths for the last twenty seconds or so. The last Echo of pull {{best_pull}} was dived and never killed, so that window ran {{last_window}} seconds anyway. The burn cost was paid and the divers were lost with it.

Fix the intermission

{{intermission_loss}} of the {{time_gap}} seconds. It is what pushes an entire Echo inside the Ritual, and what makes the seventh and eighth Echoes exist at all. Kill the Echoes of Jawae in {{kill_intermission}} seconds and the well rotation stops being a problem on its own.

Keep two teams

The alternation and the entry timing here are better than all four kill logs, and the team shape already matches theirs exactly. A third team is a stopgap for a {{best_dur_secs}}-second fight rather than the target.

Cover the intermission window

If the Ritual is still running when an Echo wakes, somebody has to go anyway. Forty-odd seconds of Grasping Depths on twenty players costs more healing than five people missing from the Jawae echoes costs damage.

Four seconds early is survivable, eight is not

Immortal Coil ramps for about 4.6s before it bites, so entering a few seconds early can cost nothing at all. Entering eight seconds early killed {{died_early}}. If somebody has to go in hot, that is the budget.
