The explosions

A purple touching another orb puts 20 Venom Rupture stacks on everyone at once. Frontals add stacks on their cast and the push adds them as stage two starts, so a jump of {{explosion_stacks}} or more, or a gain at any other moment, is an explosion. On a raid with no stacks the log records the explosion without a count ({{explosions_small}} of them), so those show as “–”; killed is the Venom Rupture deaths in the next two and a half seconds. One within {{deluge_grace}}s of a Toxic Deluge cast, with nobody carrying, is a Mutation left on the ground; the rest were touched. Contact comes from where the orbs were. Every orb on the ground is logged with a position when it appears (on a Deluge, or where a carry ends), a pickup removes the nearest one, a frontal removes as many as its Rupture stacks say it detonated (those nearest the tank) and the push removes the rest; a carried orb moves with its carrier. A contact is a carried purple reaching any orb on the ground, or a carried green reaching a purple, within {{touch_yd}} yd in the {{fresh_s}}s before the explosion, having been further off before. Orbs lying on the ground do not set each other off, and two carried orbs never met at an explosion. {{contact_named}} of {{contact_of}} touched explosions before a wipe name someone; the rest had no fresh contact the log can place. It is evidence, not a verdict: over the four nights, {{false_pct}}% of quiet 0.7-second windows with a purple out show a contact like this with no explosion, because the frontals’ cones are guessed and a carrier’s position comes from the hits they take. Holding a purple lists anyone carrying one, or who put one down, in the {{hold_s}} seconds before.

Guillotine soaks

One player is marked, and the axe lands on them five seconds later; everyone it hits shares it. If too few are under it, it becomes Execution on the whole raid (a miss). Two per stage one, at 48s and 133s; Grim Guillotine in stage three. {{g_windows}} soaks before a wipe, {{g_misses}} missed; the median soak had {{g_median_soak}} players and none with {{g_min_soak}} or more failed. Of the {{g_misses_all}} misses over the night, those after the wipe had started are a raid that had already fallen apart, not a cause.

Intermission · The Claimed Vessel

Zul’jan comes back

When stage two ends, Zul’jan is raised on 1 HP and Malacrass hides behind Deathguard. Ghastly Regeneration heals Zul’jan 2% of his health a second, {{int_regen_pct}}% over the {{int_secs}}-second intermission, and the raid’s damage is all that holds him down. Whatever he has when it ends is what he starts stage three with. {{int_pulls}} pulls got here; {{int_clean}} were played out, and Zul’jan went into stage three on {{int_z_best}}–{{int_z_worst}}%.

All through the intermission Fragments of Malacrass walk at Zul’jan. Blocked: body-block one and it is gone, at the cost of a Spirit Erasure hit on every living player (and a stack of the debuff). Missed: one that reaches him casts Reclaim Essence, which heals Zul’jan 10% of his health and Malacrass {{int_m_pct}}% of his. About {{int_fragments_each}} fragments came per intermission; {{int_missed}} got through over the night. At the raid’s median {{int_dps}} damage per second into Zul’jan, one missed fragment undoes {{int_missed_secs}} seconds of the whole raid’s damage. Spirit Erasure comes from Malacrass and hits everyone the same, so the log can’t say who blocked a fragment or whose lane one came through.

How this is read. A wipe starts at the first death of the first run of {{cascade_n}} deaths inside {{cascade_s}} seconds that the raid never recovers from (no {{recover_s}}-second stretch without a death before the pull ends); nothing after it counts unless a table says so. Alive: a player is dead from their death until they next cast something, so a battle rez brings them back. Reset pulls ({{resets}}) carry Malacrass’s Unworthy and end inside {{reset_s}} seconds; they are left out. Stage three ({{reached_p3}} pulls) has no section of its own yet: its Grim Guillotines, Blighted Severs and shields sit in the tables above with their stage marked.
