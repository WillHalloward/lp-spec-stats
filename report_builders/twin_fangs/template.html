How the fight runs

Read off this log. Both bosses are fought together, and Eternal Venom stacks are what ties the mechanics together.

Blood Torrent & globules (9s, 70s)Vexhul's Caustic Deluge drops {{glob_n}} Caustic Globules over five seconds, and Ithraz's spilled blood wraps most of them ({{shield_lo}} to {{shield_hi}} a wave this night) in a Barbed Bulwark. Crowd control strips the shield, and one player walks into each globule for one Eternal Venom stack. An unsoaked globule explodes and gives everybody a stack.
Rouse the Brood (36s, 97s)Ten Broodlings surface one by one at ten fixed spots in an arc, five per side, and start casting Visceral Burst. A kick sends one back into the water. A completed Burst hits the whole raid hard and leaves a stacking DoT.
Venomous Emergence (with Rouse)Three Spawn of Vexhul appear and give everyone a stack of Eternal Venom. Each one keeps casting Corrosive Spit lines that add stacks until it dies.
Ravenous Feast (61s, 122s)Three smashes, each split by at least four soakers. Every soak removes one Eternal Venom stack, and Feasted stops the same player soaking twice. Blood founts follow and have to be healed into within eight seconds, or Tainted Burst goes off on the raid.
Eternal VenomA permanent stacking DoT. Its ticks grow with the stacks, and guides put the one-shot at nine stacks on Mythic. On Mythic, a player who dies to it drops more globules, so one Venom death feeds the next.
Submerge (140s)Sanguine Storm, then Vexhul's Vile Flood beam sweeps the room. Every beam tick is a stack. {{flood_pulls}} pulls reached it.

Each globule is one stack for one player, so nobody should take two in a wave. Took two or more in one wave: {{soak_over}}. How this is read. A wipe starts at the first death of the first run of {{cascade_n}} deaths within {{cascade_s}} seconds; the kill, if there is one, has none. A mechanic that opens after the wipe started is not counted. Neither is a globule wave cut off by the wipe within ten seconds. A Broodling's spot is the nearest of the ten spots to where it first took damage, within six yards. A kick ready means the kicker was alive and their interrupt's cooldown was up when the Burst completed; the night's most-used interrupt counts for each player. A globule soak is a Caustic Globule hit on one player. An explosion is the other Caustic Globule spell hitting three or more players within 0.6 seconds. A stack's source is the hit that landed within 0.15 seconds of the stack. A shield strip is a Barbed Bulwark dropping within 0.4 seconds of a stun, fear or incapacitate on that globule. Healing into a fount is healing absorbed on a player while they carry Tainted Blood, leaving out anyone also under Blood Torrent's absorb.
