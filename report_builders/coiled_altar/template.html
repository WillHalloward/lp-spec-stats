Every possession makes two more ghosts. Dreadmarch is an absorb shield: it does not expire and it cannot be stunned away. A four-person cast becomes eight ghosts; one person caught becomes two, who can catch two more. The pulls that lost the intermission took a chain of mind controls rather than one bad one.
